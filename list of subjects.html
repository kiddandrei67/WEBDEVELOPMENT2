  • GS
    1. lesson 1:physics
      1. The study of matter, energy, and the fundamental laws governing the universe.
      2. Acts as the mechanical user manual for everything we see and experience.
      3. Applying mechanics principles to design safe, stable public bridges.

      1. Classifies movement into categories like linear, rotational, periodic, and oscillatory.
      2. Reveals the mathematically predictable patterns behind all moving objects around us.

      1. Newton’s laws: Inertia, Force (F = ma), and Action-Reaction.
      2. Elegant proof that a few simple rules govern vast physical realities.

  • GM
    1. lesson 1:salary,wage
      1. Salary is a fixed regular payment; wages fluctuate based on hours or pieces worked.
      2. Vital for personal budgeting and evaluating job contracts realistically.
      3. Comparing a fixed salary offer against an hourly wage to evaluate financial security.
    1. lesson 2:commission
      1. Earnings calculated as a percentage of generated sales volume.
      2. Directly links effort to reward, though it introduces income instability.
      3. A real estate agent calculating income by multiplying a house price by a 3% rate.
    1. lesson 3:overtime pay
      1. Premium compensation (e.g., 1.5x regular rate) for working past standard hours.
      2. Protects workers' personal time and ensures fair corporate compensation.
      3. Calculating extra pay: 5 hours * ($10/hr * 1.5) = $75.
      4. lesson 4:value added tax (VAT)
        1. VAT is a consumption tax assessed on the value added in each production stage of a product.
        2. It reveals how everyday consumer prices are structured and how indirect taxes flow to the government.
        3. Calculating a 12% VAT on a $100 item: $100 * 0.12 = $12 tax, making the total purchase price $112.

      2. True communication begins within; you must understand your thoughts to speak effectively.

    1. lesson 2:controlled interpersonal and uncontrolles interpersonal
      1. Controlled is planned interaction; uncontrolled is spontaneous, subconscious body language.

    1. lesson 3: language and tone
      1. Language is the choice of words used, while tone is the attitude and emotion conveyed through those words.
      2. It is a powerful reminder that "how" we say something is often much more impactful than "what" we actually say.
      3. Drafting a professional email using polite language and an empathetic tone to resolve a customer complaint.
