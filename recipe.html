Sinigang Recipe

INTRODUCTION

There is nothing more comforting to a Filipino home than the rich, savory, and intensely sour aroma of a bubbling pot of Sinigang na Baboy. This classic Filipino sour soup strikes the perfect balance between tender, melt-in-your-mouth pork belly, crisp native vegetables, and a vibrant tamarind broth.

  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 45 minutes
  • Total Time: 1 hour
  • Servings: 4-6 servings
  • Cuisine: Filipino
  • Course: Main Course

Ingredients

the base & protein
  • 1 kg pork belly or pork ribs, cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 1 large red onion, quartered
  • 3 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 2 liters water
  • 1 pack (40g) tamarind soup base mix (Sinigang mix)
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis), plus more to taste
    • The Vegetables:
    • 2 medium taro (gabi), peeled and quartered
    • 1 bunch string beans (sitaw), cut into 2-inch lengths
    • 1 medium radish (labanos), sliced diagonally
    • 2-3 pieces long green chili (siling haba)
    • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong), leaves and tender stalks separated
